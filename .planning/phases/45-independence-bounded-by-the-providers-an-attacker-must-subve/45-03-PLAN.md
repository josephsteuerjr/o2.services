**The corpus.** Build it from `trackedPaths(ROOT)` filtered to paths under `packages/` ending

**What it forbids.** Findings are, over stripped text:

1. A comparison of anything against one of the four strength literals, in either operand
   order: the four literals `owner-attested`, `owner-domain`, `single-issuer`, `independent`

2. A `case` label naming one of the four.
3. A `switch` whose discriminant is an expression ending `.strength` — the shape that would
   reintroduce the same coupling without any of the operators above.

**How the four literals are written into this file matters.** Compose them from a single
exported-shaped constant list declared once at the top with a stated reason, so the file names
each string exactly once and a fifth strength added later is one edit. Do not scatter them.

**The exemption, and only one.** `packages/core/src/quorum.ts` is exempt for its two
exhaustive switches — `attestationRank` and `describeAttestation` — because those switches are
the **definition** of the ordering and the words, not a caller comparing against them. Give the
exemption a stated reason of more than 20 characters and hold it to a **dead-exemption check**
in `vocabulary.node.test.ts`'s form: if the exempted file stops producing the findings the
exemption covers, the entry must be deleted and this case says so. Prefer a *line*-shaped
exemption over a path-shaped one, so a **new** string comparison added to `quorum.ts`
elsewhere is still a finding.

`packages/node/src/mutation-ledger.ts` must pass with **no** exemption — its `find`/`replace`/
`signature` strings are data about mutations, not comparisons, and the patterns above do not

**The corpus is the release copy and is named by path**, because it is small, fixed, and the

**What is forbidden, and the distinction is the whole difficulty.** The promise being refused is
an independent **verification / checking** claim, not the project's core-value sentence about
independently-*owned* devices. So the patterns target verbs of checking:
`independently verified`, `independent verification`, `verified independently`,
`independently checked`, `independent check` (of a result), `independent operators agreed`.
They must NOT match `independently-owned`, `independently owned`, `independent cubes`,
`independently derived`, or `an independent oracle` — every one of which is present in this
tree today and is true.

**Both controls, in the same case.** Positive: feed the checker a synthetic sentence —
*"every answer is independently verified by separate operators"* — and require a finding.
Negative: feed it `README.md`'s own core-value sentence about independently-owned nodes and the
demo's *"independent cubes"* sentence and require none. Without both, this case cannot
distinguish "the copy is clean" from "the pattern matches nothing".

**The finding is reported whichever way it comes out.** The ROADMAP's words are that this phase
must check the copy *"rather than assume it"*, and that if the copy promises independence
*"either the copy moves or the finding is reported; it is not closed by reading the copy and
deciding it is fine."* So:

- If the guard is green, the SUMMARY records the corpus, the patterns, the controls, and the
  reading — *the release copy makes no independent-verification promise; the redundancy claim it
